India's Bad-Loan Turnaround

A comprehensive analysis of how India successfully resolved its non-performing assets (NPA) crisis and revitalized its banking system.

The NPA Crisis Background

  • Twin Balance Sheet Problem: Over-leveraged corporations and stressed public sector banks.
  • Peak NPAs: Gross NPAs reached over 11.2% around 2017-2018.

Key Recovery Frameworks

  • IBC 2016: Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code provided time-bound resolution.
  • Asset Reconstruction (NARCL): Bad bank structure to aggregate stressed assets.

4R Strategy

1. Recognition: Clean up balance sheets transparently.

2. Resolution: Speedy recovery through IBC.

3. Recapitalization: Infusing capital into PSBs.

4. Reforms: Governance and credit risk enhancement.

Current Situation & Impact

  • Gross NPAs dropped significantly below 3% in recent years.
  • Bank balance sheets are now stronger with healthy credit growth.

Future Outlook

Sustaining risk underwriting standards, monitoring retail loans, and digital risk analytics to prevent future distress cycles.
