India's Bad-Loan Turnaround

Sub-topic: Classifying Banking Assets, Then & Now

An overview of how Indian public and private sector banks transitioned from peak non-performing assets (NPAs) to robust balance sheets through regulatory and structural reforms.

Presenter: Rehan Shaikh (37)

Understanding Asset Classification

  • Standard Assets: Regular repayments without default risks.
  • Sub-standard Assets: NPA for less than or equal to 12 months.
  • Doubtful / Loss Assets: High risk of non-recovery requiring provisioning.
Presenter: Rehan Shaikh (37)

Historical Gross NPA Trends

Presenter: Trushty Shirgaonkar (41)

The "Then" Era: The NPA Peak

Unchecked credit growth post-2008 led to a surge in bad loans. Peak Gross NPA ratio reached 11.2% in FY18, creating severe capital stress across Public Sector Banks (PSBs).

Presenter: Trushty Shirgaonkar (41)

Asset Share During Peak Crisis

Presenter: Babita Rana (65)

Regulatory Interventions (4R Strategy)

1. Recognition: Asset Quality Review (AQR) by RBI.

2. Resolution: Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016.

3. Recapitalization: Capital infusion into PSBs.

4. Reforms: Better governance and risk management.

Presenter: Babita Rana (65)

Role of IBC Code 2016

  • Time-bound resolution framework for defaulting companies.
  • Significantly improved creditor bargaining power and recovery rates.
Presenter: Anuja Patil (27)

The "Now" Era: Recovery & Clean Balance Sheets

Gross NPAs dropped below 3% recently, marking a multi-decade low due to write-offs, recoveries, and prudent lending strategies.

Presenter: Anuja Patil (27)

Current Asset Share Distribution

Presenter: Anushka Sonawane (47)

Impact on Banking Profitability

  • PSBs recorded net profits exceeding ₹1 trillion in recent financial cycles.
  • Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR) improved to over 75%.
Presenter: Anushka Sonawane (47)

NARCL & Bad Bank Mechanism

National Asset Reconstruction Company Ltd. (NARCL) aggregates bad loans to free up bank operational capacity for new credit extension.

Presenter: Soumya Manjrekar (60)

Future Outlook & Risk Analytics

Shift towards digital credit underwriting, early warning signals (EWS), and monitoring retail/MSME portfolios to prevent fresh slippages.

Presenter: Soumya Manjrekar (60)

Key Takeaways

  • India successfully navigated from a high-NPA trap to balance sheet resilience.
  • Strong regulatory framework ensures long-term macroeconomic stability.
