India's Bad-Loan Turnaround

Classifying Banking Assets, Then & Now

Background

Indian banking suffered severe Non-Performing Asset (NPA) stress between 2012–2018. Over-leveraged corporates and delayed recoveries degraded balance sheets.

The Transformation

Through policy reforms, Asset Quality Reviews (AQR), and the IBC framework, India executed one of the largest banking recovery turnarounds.

Rehan Shaikh | Roll No: 37
Slide 4 / 17

Understanding Non-Performing Assets (NPAs)

What is an Asset in Banking?

Loans given by banks are their assets because they generate interest income. When borrowers stop paying, loans turn into "Bad Loans".

NPA Definition Criteria

An asset becomes Non-Performing when principal or interest payment remains overdue for more than 90 days for commercial loans.

Classification of Banking Assets

Standard Assets

Performing assets with regular interest payments and low default risk.

Sub-Standard Assets

Assets remaining as NPAs for less than or equal to 12 months.

Doubtful & Loss Assets

Doubtful: NPA > 12 months.
Loss: Identified as uncollectible by RBI audit.

The "Then" Scenario (2012 - 2018 Crisis)

The Twin Balance Sheet Problem

Over-leveraged infrastructure companies + stressed Public Sector Banks unable to lend fresh capital.

Peak NPA Level

In FY 2018, Gross NPAs hit a record high of 11.2% of total loans in India, threatening economic stability.

Key Drivers of the NPA Crisis

Aggressive Lending Practices

Unchecked credit growth during the 2004–2008 boom period without proper risk evaluation.

Policy Delays & Global Factors

Stalled power & infrastructure projects combined with the global commodity slowdown after 2011.

Regulatory Interventions: RBI's Clean-Up

Asset Quality Review (AQR) 2015

RBI forced banks to stop evergreen/hidden loans and transparently declare actual stressed balance sheets.

Prompt Corrective Action (PCA)

Weak banks were placed under lending restrictions to prevent further capital erosion.

Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC) Gamechanger

Shift in Power

Shifted control from Debtor-in-Control to Creditor-in-Control, speeding up corporate resolutions.

Recovery Speed

Over ₹3+ Lakh Crores recovered through IBC resolution processes, drastically enhancing recovery rates.

The Bad Bank Concept (NARCL)

What is NARCL?

National Asset Reconstruction Company Ltd (NARCL) set up to aggregate and clean up toxic loans above ₹500 Crores.

Government Guarantee Structure

15% paid in Cash, 85% in Security Receipts backed by Govt guarantee for smooth debt resolution.

Government Recapitalisation Strategy

Capital Infusion

Government injected over ₹3.1 Lakh Crores into Public Sector Banks (PSBs) to maintain capital adequacy (CRAR).

Mega Consolidation of PSBs

Merged 27 Public Sector Banks into 12 strong, capital-rich, and technology-driven national anchor banks.

The "Now" Scenario: Historic NPA Recovery

Gross NPA Drop

Gross NPAs dropped from 11.2% (FY18) to a decade low of ~2.8% (FY24/25).

Net NPA Drop

Net NPAs reached below 0.6%, representing world-class provision coverage ratios across Indian banks.
